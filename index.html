The problem

Governance moves faster than manual review.

Important proposals can contain fabricated claims, scam mechanics, or material inconsistencies. A single opinion is difficult to audit and easy to miss.

01

Signal is scattered

Reviewers need to combine proposal text, independent analysis, and payment evidence before making a decision.

02

Confidence is unclear

One Miner can be wrong. Agreement across independent assessments makes escalation criteria visible.

03

Evidence gets lost

Without an append-only record, teams cannot easily retrace what was asked, paid for, or verified.

Built for responsible operation

Safety is part of the product.

Sentinel makes the limits and provenance visible so usage can be demonstrated without padding traffic or hiding uncertainty.

Hard budget guard

Stops before the first request that would exceed 100 completed requests or the configured $1 ceiling.

No-repeat ledger

Records completed requests in an append-only local ledger and avoids reprocessing a proposal already recorded.

Receipt-backed evidence

Only verified payment receipts and signal hashes become Layer 1 evidence. Missing evidence stays distinguishable from fraud.
